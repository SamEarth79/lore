# Wireframe spec: Lore — self-healing company brain

> Generated by `wireframe.md` during `/design` from the approved HTML files
> in this directory. Regenerate from the HTML after any change — never
> hand-edit this file, or it drifts from the wireframe it describes.
> This is the contract `/implement` is held to: `draft.md` writes story
> acceptance criteria against it, `frontend` reproduces it, `execute.md`'s
> conformance check, `qa`'s Playwright selectors, and `reviewer`'s verdict
> all check against the Element index at the bottom.

Approved: 2026-10-16
Source files: app-shell.html, ask.html, inbox.html, freshness.html, ingest.html

Global conventions (all screens):
- Freshness badge copy (one `FreshnessBadge` component, icon + text, never colour alone):
  `✓ Trusted` · `⚠ Suspect` · `💬 Asking {person}` · `◌ Unverified` · `❓ Nobody knows` ·
  `Superseded` (struck-through). Precedence: superseded > nobody knows > asking > suspect >
  unverified > trusted. The value comes from the API's `freshness` field — never re-derived
  client-side.
- All user/ingested/LLM text renders as plain text (architecture rendering contract).
- Every mutation response's `graphVersion` triggers refetch of the visible view.

### States
- **empty** — tagline, empty prompt, 6 suggestions (incl. one real-repo and one "not in the graph"), input, graph placeholder.
